About Me

My Story

I've always had a passion for technology and video games. Growing up, I spent countless hours playing video games, and my curiosity about how software works eventually developed into an interest in programming.

I first became interested in coding during my initial bachelor's degree, but personal circumstances prevented me from pursuing that interest at the time. In 2022, an opportunity to continue my education through Target's education assistance program allowed me to return to that goal and pursue a bachelor's degree in Web Development.

Education

I hold a bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering from Universidad Nacional Experimental Politécnica Antonio José de Sucre (UNEXPO). I am currently completing my bachelor's degree in Web Development at Bellevue University.

Professional Background

I have more than eight years of experience in logistics and warehouse operations, including inventory control, operational coordination, and yard management at Target. This experience has helped me develop strong problem-solving, communication, and organizational skills.

My goal is to combine my experience in logistics and industrial engineering with software development to create practical solutions that improve business operations.
